<Update label="October 2026" description="Sound Generator Pro" tags={["New model", "Free in October", "Pricing", "Limits", "Errors"]}>
  ### Sound Generator Pro is live
  Our fastest, highest-quality sound model now powers every [Sound Generator](/api-documentation/conversions/soundgenerator) request, with no new parameters needed.
  - Generate **sound FX**, **loops at any BPM**, **instrument samples**, and **Foley** from one prompt.
  - Set `audio_length` from **5 seconds to 2 minutes**. Prompts can be up to **300 characters**.

  ### Free for the whole month of October
  Sound Generator is **free throughout October**, up to **500 generations/day** and **20 generations/hour**.

  ### New Sound Generator pricing
  After October, Sound Generator costs **more than 3x less**: from **\$0.011/generation** on the Pro plan (was \$0.037). See the [Pricing Page](/api-documentation/index/pricing).

  ### Input audio length limits
  - **4 minutes**: `v1/Remix`, Extend, Inpaint, and Sing Over Instrumental
  - **6 minutes**: `v2/Remix`
  - **8 minutes**: all other features

  See [Audio Length Limits](/api-documentation/utilities/ratelimits#audio-length-limits).

  ### Error codes and webhooks
  - Error responses now include an `error_code` (for example `INSUFFICIENT_CREDITS`). See [Handling Errors](/api-documentation/utilities/error).
  - Every conversion webhook now includes a `title` field.
  - Output files are served from our CDN. `audio_url` is valid for 1 day.
</Update>
